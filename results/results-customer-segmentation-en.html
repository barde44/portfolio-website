Customer Segmentation by Clustering

Tools: Python (pandas, numpy, scikit-learn, matplotlib, seaborn, scipy) Duration: ~6 hours

Business Problem

A shopping mall wants to better understand its customers to personalize marketing campaigns. With 200 customers described by 5 variables (gender, age, annual income, spending score), the goal is to identify homogeneous groups of customers with similar behaviors. Unsupervised clustering reveals these segments without predefined labels.

What customer profiles emerge naturally? How can marketing strategies be adapted to each segment?

Project Objectives

  • Analyze the distributions and relationships between customer variables
  • Compare 3 clustering algorithms: K-Means, Agglomerative Clustering (Ward), DBSCAN
  • Determine the optimal number of clusters (Elbow method + Silhouette)
  • Visualize segments in 2D (PCA) and 3D
  • Interpret each segment's profile
  • Formulate marketing recommendations per segment

Key Insights

Algorithm Comparison

  • K-Means provides the best separation (Silhouette = 0.3979) with balanced clusters
  • Agglomerative Clustering (Ward) produces similar results (Silhouette = 0.3584) with the added benefit of the dendrogram
  • DBSCAN is unsuitable here: 25.5% outliers, fragmentation into 8 micro-clusters

Customer Structure

  • The largest segment is Premium Customers (28.5%): high income and high spending
  • Budget Spenders (19%) represent a paradox: low income but high propensity to spend
  • Wealthy Savers (14%) underutilize their purchasing power — marketing opportunity

Dimensionality Reduction

  • PCA 2D captures ~76% of variance with only 2 components
  • Projections visually confirm the quality of the K-Means segmentation

Recommendations

  1. Target Premium Customers (28.5%) with exclusive offers and a VIP loyalty program
  2. Activate Wealthy Savers (14%) through personalized campaigns to stimulate spending
  3. Retain Budget Spenders (19%) with promotional offers and credit options
  4. Offer entry-level deals to Cautious Customers (20%) to increase engagement
  5. Use K-Means as the reference algorithm for operational segmentation
  6. Update the segmentation quarterly with new customer data
