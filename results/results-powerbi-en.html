Power BI Dashboard — Global Superstore

Tools: Power BI Desktop, DAX, Power Query Duration: ~4 hours

Business Problem

A global distribution company (Global Superstore) wants to track its commercial performance across all international markets. With over 25,000 orders spread across 7 markets and 3 customer segments, management needs an interactive dashboard to monitor sales, profitability and geographic revenue distribution.

"How are our $12.64M in sales distributed across markets, categories and time periods? What levers can improve the 11.61% margin?"

Project Objectives

  • Build an interactive dashboard in Power BI Desktop
  • Calculate and display key KPIs (revenue, profit, margin, order count)
  • Visualize sales trends over time (2011-2014)
  • Analyze sales by product category and by country
  • Implement interactive filters (Segment, Market, Category)
  • Enable dynamic data exploration for decision-makers

The Dataset

Source: Global Superstore (worldwide distribution dataset)

  • 25,000+ orders over 4 years (2011-2014)
  • 7 international markets (Africa, APAC, Canada, EMEA, EU, LATAM, US)
  • 3 customer segments (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office)
  • 3 product categories (Technology, Furniture, Office Supplies)

Key Insights

Overall Performance

  • Total revenue of $12.64M with $1.47M profit (11.61% margin)
  • 25,000+ orders processed over 2011-2014
  • Continuous sales growth: from ~$2M in 2011 to ~$4.5M in 2014

Category Analysis

  • Technology leads in sales (~$4.7M), followed by Furniture (~$4.1M) and Office Supplies (~$3.8M)
  • All three categories have comparable volumes, with Technology slightly ahead

Geographic Distribution

  • Worldwide distribution across 7 markets
  • Strong concentration in North America, Europe and APAC
  • Africa and LATAM represent emerging markets to develop

Time Trend

  • Steady upward trend over 4 years
  • Notable acceleration between 2013 and 2014
  • Revenue more than doubled from 2011 (~$2M) to 2014 (~$4.5M)

Recommendations

  1. Deep-dive into Technology: leading category, explore most profitable sub-categories
  2. Optimize margin (11.61%): identify products/markets with negative margins
  3. Develop emerging markets: LATAM and Africa show growth potential
  4. Analyze discount impact: excessive discounts may explain the moderate margin
