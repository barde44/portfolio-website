ReviewScope -- Consumer Review Scraping and Analysis

Tools: Python, BeautifulSoup, requests, SQLite, Streamlit, pandas Duration: ~10 hours

Business problem

A marketing team wants to analyze consumer reviews on tech products to identify satisfaction trends, compare brands and detect recurring pain points.

Project objectives

  • Scrape product reviews from e-commerce websites using BeautifulSoup
  • Clean and structure extracted data (text, ratings, dates, verification status)
  • Design a SQLite schema with 3 tables, 6 indexes and 3 analytical views
  • Implement 8 advanced analytical SQL queries
  • Build an interactive bilingual Streamlit dashboard (FR/EN)

Scraper architecture

The scraper uses BeautifulSoup to parse HTML pages and extract reviews via multiple CSS selectors. Each review is cleaned (whitespace removal, unicode normalization) and structured into 7 fields: author, rating, title, content, date, helpfulness count and verification status.

A demo mode generates realistic data for 12 products across 5 categories (Audio, Smartphones, Laptops, TV, Home), with review templates adapted by category and rating.

Database schema

  • sources: e-commerce websites (Amazon, Best Buy, Walmart) with URL
  • products: name, category, brand, source, average rating, review count
  • reviews: author, rating (CHECK 1-5), title, content, date, helpfulness, verification

6 indexes optimize joins and filters: idx_reviews_product, idx_reviews_rating, idx_reviews_date, idx_reviews_product_rating, idx_products_source, idx_products_category.

Analytical views

  • v_product_summary: product summary with review count, average rating, positive/negative/neutral
  • v_rating_distribution: rating distribution per product with percentage
  • v_monthly_activity: monthly activity per product (review count and average rating)

SQL analytical queries

  1. Product overview with rating summary
  2. Rating distribution per product with percentage
  3. Most helpful reviews (sorted by helpful_count)
  4. Monthly trends (volume and average rating)
  5. Sentiment analysis by category (positive/negative %)
  6. Review length vs rating correlation
  7. Verified vs unverified purchase comparison
  8. Brand ranking by satisfaction

Interactive dashboard

The Streamlit application offers 5 analysis tabs:

  • Dashboard: 6 KPIs, product table with ratings and stars, charts by category and rating distribution
  • Products: category filter, brand ranking, detailed rating distribution
  • Reviews: product selection, review cards with stars, [Verified] badge, helpfulness counter
  • Insights: monthly trends, verified vs unverified comparison, review length by rating
  • SQL Console: custom read-only query execution with table display
