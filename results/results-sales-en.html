Superstore Sales Dashboard — Excel

Main tool: Microsoft Excel (Power Query, Pivot Tables, Charts, Slicers) Duration: ~5 hours

Business Problem

A large American retail chain (Superstore) sells furniture, office supplies, and technology products across the United States. The sales director has 9,994 order lines spanning 4 years (2014-2017) but no clear visibility into overall performance.

"Which regions perform best? Which products lose money? Are we growing? How can we optimize our decisions?"

The goal is to transform this raw data into an interactive dashboard for quick decision-making.

Project Objectives

  • Clean and structure data with Power Query
  • Calculate 9 key business KPIs (revenue, profit, margin, average basket, etc.)
  • Create 5 pivot tables for multi-dimensional analysis
  • Build an interactive dashboard with charts and slicers
  • Identify actionable insights for management
  • Publish the project on GitHub with professional documentation

Methodology

1. Import & Cleaning (Power Query)

The CSV file had several critical issues:

  • Misinterpreted dates (US format MM/DD/YYYY vs FR format)
  • Decimal numbers stored as text (separator . vs ,)
  • Incorrectly split rows due to commas in product names

Solution: used Power Query with explicit specification of the "English (United States)" locale for date and decimal conversion.

Key Business Insights

Regions

  • West is the top-performing region (31.6% of revenue, 14.9% margin)
  • Central has the lowest margin (7.9%) despite significant volume — investigation needed

Loss-Making Products Identified

  • Tables: -$17,725 in losses (-8.6% margin!)
  • Bookcases: -$3,473 in losses (-3% margin)
  • Supplies: -$1,189 in losses (-2.5% margin)

→ These 3 sub-categories collectively lose over $22,000 for the company.

Hidden Gems to Promote

  • Labels (44% margin) and Paper (43% margin): highly profitable, undervalued products
  • Copiers (37% margin): low volume but enormous profitability
  • Canon imageCLASS 2200 alone generates $25,200 in profit (8.8% of total profit)

Time Trends

  • Impressive growth: +51% revenue between 2014 and 2017
  • Profit multiplied by 2.7 over the same period
  • Flat 2014-2015, then sharp increase in 2016-2017

Business Recommendations

  1. Immediate Action: audit why Tables, Bookcases and Supplies are loss-making (discounts? logistics costs? product mix?)
  2. Marketing: promote Labels, Paper and Copiers — highly profitable, underexploited products
  3. Geography: investigate the low margin in the Central region
  4. Customer Strategy: strengthen presence in Consumer West/East segment (47% of revenue)
  5. Monitoring: update this dashboard monthly to drive strategy
