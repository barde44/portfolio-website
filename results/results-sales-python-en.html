Sales Performance Analysis — Python

Tools: Python (pandas, matplotlib, seaborn, scipy) Duration: ~4 hours

Business Problem

A retail store (Superstore) wants to analyze its sales performance over 4 years (2014-2017) to understand which products, regions and segments are most profitable, and identify loss-making sub-categories and the impact of discounts on profitability.

"Which products lose money? Are discounts profitable? Which regions underperform?"

Project Objectives

  • Explore and clean the Superstore dataset with pandas
  • Calculate business KPIs (revenue, profit, margin, average basket)
  • Create 6 visualizations (bar plots, line charts, scatter, heatmap)
  • Analyze the impact of discounts on profitability by tier
  • Identify loss-making sub-categories (Top & Flop)
  • Test statistical correlations with Pearson (scipy)

Key Business Insights

Overall Performance

  • Total revenue of $2.3M with $286K profit (12.5% margin)
  • Technology is the most profitable category (15.6% margin)
  • Furniture has high revenue but near-zero profit (3.9% margin)

Loss-Making Sub-categories

  • Tables: -$17,725 in losses (26% average discount, -14.8% margin)
  • Bookcases: -$3,473 in losses (21% average discount)
  • Total losses: -$22,387 across 3 sub-categories

Discount Impact

  • Discounts 0-10%: profitable (average profit +$65)
  • Discounts > 20%: systematically unprofitable
  • Discount ↔ Margin_Pct correlation: r = -0.86 (very strong)

Recommendations

  1. Cap discounts at 20%: beyond this, every sale destroys margin
  2. Revise pricing for Tables & Bookcases: these sub-categories lose money despite significant revenue
  3. Boost Technology in Central: underexploited region for the most profitable category
  4. Monitor orders with Discount > 30%: set up automatic alerts
