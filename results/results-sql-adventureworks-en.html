Advanced SQL — AdventureWorks Performance

Tools: SQL, SQLite, Python (pandas, matplotlib, seaborn) Duration: ~6 hours

Business Problem

A manufacturing company (AdventureWorks) wants to analyze its commercial performance over the 2022-2024 period. With 3,000 orders, 500 customers across 9 regions and 30 products in 4 categories, management needs advanced SQL queries to extract strategic insights.

"Which products, regions and segments generate the most revenue? What levers can improve the overall margin?"

Project Objectives

  • Create a relational SQLite database with 9 normalized tables
  • Write 15 advanced SQL queries (joins, aggregations, window functions, subqueries, CASE WHEN)
  • Create 3 SQL views to automate KPI reporting
  • Analyze sales by region, category, agent, segment and product
  • Build a summary dashboard with 6 charts and 4 KPIs

The 15 SQL Queries

Basic Queries (1-3)

  • Sales by region 2024: JOIN Region + SUM/COUNT aggregation
  • Active customers: HAVING + MAX(OrderDate)
  • Sales by category: JOIN 4 tables + average basket

Aggregations and Relationships (4-6)

  • Sales by agent: LEFT JOIN + multi-table GROUP BY
  • Top 5 profitable products: Profit calculation (ListPrice - StandardCost)
  • Sales by segment: Window Function SUM() OVER() for percentage shares

Customer Segment Analysis (7-9)

  • Customer segmentation: CASE WHEN + subquery (Low/Medium/High)
  • Top 10 customers: RANK() OVER (ORDER BY ...)
  • Online vs Store: Pivot with MAX(CASE WHEN ...)

Product Analysis (10-12)

  • Popular products 2024: Temporal filtering + ORDER BY quantity
  • Sales by subcategory: 12 detailed subcategories
  • Cumulative monthly sales: SUM() OVER (ROWS BETWEEN UNBOUNDED PRECEDING AND CURRENT ROW)

Sales Optimization (13-15)

  • Region x Product: Two-dimension cross-analysis
  • Return rates: LEFT JOIN + COALESCE + NULLIF
  • Product profitability: NTILE(4) quartiles + net margin

Key Insights

Category Analysis

  • Components leads in sales ($41,259), followed by Clothing ($35,408), Accessories ($33,539) and Bikes ($30,034)
  • Handlebars ($17,232) and Bottles ($14,232) subcategories dominate in revenue

Customer Segmentation

  • Individual: 64% of sales (318 customers, 1,797 orders)
  • Store: 36% of sales (179 customers, 999 orders)
  • Most active customers combine both Online and Store purchases

Regional Performance (2024)

  • Central leads ($5,769), followed by Northeast ($5,459) and Southeast ($5,152)
  • Relatively balanced distribution across all 9 regions

Return Analysis

  • High return rates: Mountain-200 Silver (62%), HL Handlebar (62%)
  • Indicates quality issues or product-customer fit problems

Recommendations

  1. Audit high-return products: Mountain-200 Silver (62%) and HL Handlebar (62%) require quality analysis
  2. Grow the Store segment: 36% of sales with significant improvement potential
  3. Optimize pricing structure: ListPrice/StandardCost gap needs review on certain products
  4. Strengthen underperforming regions: France and Northwest lag behind in 2024
  5. Automate reporting: the 3 SQL views enable real-time KPI tracking
