SQL Sales Reports — Chinook Database

Tools: SQL (SQLite), Python (matplotlib) Duration: ~3 hours

Business Problem

A digital music store wants to analyze its sales from its relational database (Chinook) to understand which countries, music genres and artists generate the most revenue, and evaluate sales agent performance.

"Which countries generate the most revenue? Which music genres dominate? Which artists are most profitable?"

Project Objectives

  • Explore the Chinook database with structured SQL queries
  • Calculate global KPIs (revenue, invoices, average basket)
  • Analyze sales by country, music genre and artist
  • Identify top customers and evaluate employee performance
  • Produce a summary dashboard with matplotlib

Key Business Insights

Overall Performance

  • Total revenue of $2,328.60 across 412 invoices
  • Low average basket ($5.65) → typical of digital (per-track purchases)
  • 59 customers across 24 countries

Geographic Analysis

  • USA dominates with $523.06 (22.5% of total revenue, 13 customers)
  • Top 5 countries = 59% of total revenue (USA, Canada, France, Brazil, Germany)
  • Strong geographic concentration of revenue

Music Genres

  • Rock = 36% of sales (835 tracks out of 2,240)
  • Top 4 genres (Rock, Latin, Metal, Alt & Punk) = 79% of sales
  • TV Shows 5th by revenue ($93.53) despite only 47 sales (higher unit price)

Recommendations

  1. Strengthen the US market: 22.5% of revenue but growth potential
  2. Capitalize on Rock: 36% of sales, expand Rock/Metal catalog
  3. Increase average basket: bundles, full album promotions vs individual tracks
  4. Develop European markets: France, Germany, UK show good potential
  5. Analyze TV Shows: high revenue/sale ($1.99 vs $0.99) → push this format
