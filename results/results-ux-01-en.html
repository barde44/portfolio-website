Business Website Redesign — Health Plus Clinic

Main tool: Figma Program: Future Interns — UI/UX Design Internship

Business Problem

A local health clinic (Health Plus Clinic) has an outdated business website: not responsive, slow loading time, and low conversion rate on the contact form. Mobile visitors cannot navigate properly.

"How to transform this aging website into an effective patient acquisition tool, accessible on all devices?"

Project Objectives

  • Create personas and user journeys
  • Create low-fidelity wireframes to structure navigation
  • Design high-fidelity responsive mockups on Figma (6 pages)
  • Apply a mobile-first approach with desktop adaptation
  • Simplify the contact form to 3 fields
  • Integrate a visible call-to-action on every page

Key UX Insights

Mobile-First

  • Mobile-first approach ensures optimal experience on the most constrained device
  • Desktop adaptation naturally follows from a well-structured mobile base

Form Simplification

  • A 3-field form reduces appointment booking friction
  • A visible "Make an appointment" CTA guides the user

Visual Identity

  • Professional blue palette that inspires trust and seriousness (healthcare)
  • Service cards with icons for quick scanning
